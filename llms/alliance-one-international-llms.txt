# Alliance One International

> Alliance One International was a global leaf tobacco merchant that purchased, processed, and sold leaf tobacco to manufacturers of cigarettes and other consumer tobacco products. Formed in 2005 by the merger of Dimon Incorporated and Standard Commercial Corporation, it became one of the world's two largest leaf tobacco dealers, sourcing from the United States, Brazil, Zimbabwe, Malawi, Turkey and other growing regions. In 2018 the company renamed itself Pyxus International, Inc. as part of a diversification strategy beyond tobacco into agricultural solutions, hemp and e-liquids. Alliance One International never operated a public developer program, and its successor does not publish a machine-readable API contract.
